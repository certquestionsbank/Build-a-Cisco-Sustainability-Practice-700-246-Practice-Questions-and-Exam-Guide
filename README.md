The reviewed Cisco page and exam-topics document do not specify a question count or passing score. Do not use the size of a practice-question collection to estimate either figure. This article uses the dedicated page and topics document wording and retains the exam code as the clearest identifier.

## Official exam topics covered

The five weighted domains below come from Cisco's 700-246 CESPB exam-topics document. The percentages total 100%; they are not fixed counts of questions on an individual exam.

### 1. Building a Sustainability Practice, 25%

Candidates should be able to describe why a sustainability practice is built, understand the importance of adapting business practices, and describe sustainability practices alongside the circular economy. A useful preparation exercise is to explain what changes in a business conversation when sustainability becomes a stated customer priority.

### 2. Making a Difference, 35%

This is the largest published domain. The objectives name Cisco circular offerings, the Cisco Product Takeback and Reuse Program, customer recycling solutions, Cisco Refresh, and Cisco Green Pay. Study each named offering or program on its own terms, then practise distinguishing them in a customer scenario. The blueprint names them but does not supply a full feature comparison; use current Cisco training material for those details.

### 3. Cisco Solutions that Support Sustainability, 25%

The two objectives address sustainability and AI-ready data centers, then sustainability and future-proofed workplaces. In a practice scenario, identify the customer's intended outcome before choosing which of these solution contexts is relevant. Do not infer an unstated product specification from the broad objective headings.

### 4. Talking to Customers about Sustainability, 10%

The tasks are to research customer priorities, start a sustainability conversation, and find the right contacts. These are sequential skills, but the right opening depends on the customer. Preparation should include explaining why a particular priority or stakeholder is relevant instead of relying on a memorized pitch.

### 5. Sustainability Within Industries, 5%

The final domain asks candidates to identify digital-transformation opportunities and explain examples of sustainability journeys using Cisco networking, IoT, and security solutions. Connect the example to an industry need. The objective does not require inventing an industry-specific case study or numerical environmental benefit.

## A practical preparation path

Start with the Cisco 700-246 CESPB exam-topics document and make a checklist of all fifteen numbered subobjectives. Use the official Stage 2: Partner Environmental Sustainability Fundamentals course for the concepts and Cisco's current materials for the named programs. Cisco Partners can also use SalesConnect training content, as described on the exam page.

Study the first domain to establish the business purpose, then devote careful attention to the 35% circular-offerings domain. Move to solution contexts and customer conversations. Finish by explaining an industry example without adding unsupported details. This order is an editorial study plan, not a Cisco-required course sequence.

For each objective, prepare one short scenario. State the customer priority, the choice you would make, and what information you would still need. The exercise is especially useful where answer choices might confuse a Cisco program with a broader sustainability concept.

## Six final preparation tips

1. Keep exam code 700-246 CESPB visible in your notes because Cisco sources use different expanded names.
2. Study all five domains; the 35% section deserves attention but does not replace the other 65%.
3. Distinguish Cisco Refresh, Cisco Green Pay, takeback and reuse, and customer recycling using official explanations.
4. Link every solution discussion to a stated customer priority.
5. Practise explaining why an option does not fit, not only why your preferred answer does.
6. Recheck the current Cisco exam page and topics document before booking.
